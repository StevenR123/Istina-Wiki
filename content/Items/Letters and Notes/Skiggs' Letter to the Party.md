You have one month to bring me one of Dulmis' Tears or the heads of Cedric and whichever rat among the four of you was working with him.

If you bring me either I will let those of you remain leave without being hunted for this offense for the rest of your short lives. If not or if you attack any of my men I will find whatever passes for friends or family for all of you and have them thrown into the Shroud.
