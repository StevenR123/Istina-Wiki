# History

Wagram was originally founded by the [[Sanguine Blade]]. Later it became the capital of a larger empire by the same name. The city of Wagram was eventually conquered by the [[Sleeping Titan]] and his army at which point the empire of Wagram began to fall into many smaller states.

Since [[Dulmis' Sacrifice]] Wagram as remained mostly intact, but isolated within [[The Shroud]]. The old houses of [[The Nobility of Wagram]] have slowly been losing political power to [[Forgers|The Forgers]].

The old houses have lost all their assets and income from sources not entirely contained with Wagram while [[Forgers|The Forgers]] have been advancing their craft and providing invaluable things to the city.
# Factions
## [[Forgers|The Forgers]]

The Forgers of Wagram are a guild of arcane crafters. They are the ones who turn [[Dulmis' Tears]] into artifacts of power.

They are the single most influential group within Wagram and have a near monopoly on arcane artifacts.

Recently they have discovered a way to replicate the barrier around [[Bastion Cities]] against The Shroud on a smaller scale through [[Shroud Beacon|Shroud Beacons]]. These creations have allowed them to build the first known permanent settlement outside of a [[Bastion Cities|Bastion City]] named [[Kovati]].
## [[Titan Guard]]

The Titan guard was originally the remnants of the Sleeping Titan's army when he conquered Wagram centuries before Dulmis' Sacrifice. The were an occupying force made up almost exclusively by Orcs.

In the time since as the Titan slept they slowly integrated into the city as residents and morphed into something closer to a city guard force. Although their origin as followers of the Sleeping Titan has remained a strong point of pride they now include many non-orcs among their ranks so long as they are devotees of the Titan.
## [[The Nobility of Wagram]]

Nobility in Wagram is and always has been a statement of wealth rather than divine right. Each of the noble houses bought and maintains a Noble Charter from the governing counsel that gives them the rights and responsibilities of nobility.

These Nobles are the only members of the city that have direct control of the governance of Wagram. To join the governing counsel one must be a noble.

Each house generally has a patron deity. Most houses have The [[Sanguine Blade]] as their patron.
## [[The Travelers]]

The Travelers are a church that worship [[The Traveler]] and none of the other members of the Pantheon. They are the longest surviving organization in Wagram and have existed long before [[Dulmis' Sacrifice]] or even the [[Sleeping Titan|Sleeping Titan's]] invasion.

In the Last Age they were masters of teleportation and exploration. Their churches connected an entire empire with stable portals and they often obtained items of great power that had no known origin.

The current church is dedicated to rediscovering the arts of teleportation and portal making.
# Pantheon

The Pantheon of Wagram is seemingly made up historical figures from the distant past. Many of which had no overlap as far as records show.
## [[Sanguine Blade]]

Known as the founder of Wagram The [[Sanguine Blade]] was more of a myth in the Last Age and was not thought of as a historical figure.

The myth surrounding them says they were a fallen child of a god that choose to live as a mortal rather than a god. They conquered a wilderness and offered protection to those brave enough to help them tame the land.

They eventually founded Wagram and it as the first and only King. Many of the traditions such as the nobility system of Wagram are attributed to them. Many of the [[The Nobility of Wagram]] maintain them as their patron deity.
