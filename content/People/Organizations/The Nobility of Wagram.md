Nobility in Wagram is and always has been a statement of wealth rather than divine right. Each of the noble houses bought and maintains a Noble Charter from the governing counsel that gives them the rights and responsibilities of nobility.

These Nobles are the only members of the city that have direct control of the governance of Wagram. To join the governing counsel one must be a noble.

Each house generally has a patron deity. Most houses have The [[Sanguine Blade]] as their patron.

# Levels of Nobility
## Knight
The lowest of Nobles.
The Knight Charter is the cheapest to purchase and their ranks are generally filled with prosperous merchants, prolific craftsmen, or Awakened adventurers. Often given to the lesser children of a noble house once the heir inherits their parents charter.

### Rights
Eligibility for the governing counsel.
Right to attend and speak at counsel meetings.
### Responsibilities
The cost to maintain the Charter is equal to the cost to purchase paid every 5 years.
