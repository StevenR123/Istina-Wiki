Secret shadow organization with the [[Forgers]]

## Whisperwrights
### Access
- **Knowledge:** Restricted access to classified information about the guild’s covert operations, rival factions, and black-market dealings.
- **Resources:** Special funding for covert missions, access to illegal magical materials, and tools tailored for clandestine activities.
- **Contacts:** Networks with external agents, black-market suppliers, and informants.
### Role
Whisperwrights work to eliminate threats to the guild’s monopoly by any means necessary. This includes:

- Spying on rivals or potential competitors.
- Sabotaging unauthorized magical crafting operations.
- Silencing dissidents within or outside the guild.
- Smuggling forbidden materials or items to and from secret locations.
- Bribing or intimidating authorities to protect the guild’s interests.

Their presence within the guild is kept secret from all but the highest-ranking members (Master Forger and above). Those assigned this role must be adept at deception, blending seamlessly into their primary rank while executing their covert duties.
