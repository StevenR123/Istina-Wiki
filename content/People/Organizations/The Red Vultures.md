# Summary
The Red Vultures are a small mercenary band specializing in beast hunting. They send expeditions into [[The Shroud]] to find and harvest specific [[Awakening#Animals|Beasts]]. This is usually done to provide crafters specific magical parts of these beasts, but occasionally they are contracted for extermination if a large population of dangerous or Nuisance beasts appears.
