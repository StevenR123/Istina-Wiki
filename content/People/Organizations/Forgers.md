![[Forgers.png]]

The Forgers of [[Wagram]] are a guild of arcane crafters. They are the ones who turn [[Dulmis' Tears]] into artifacts of power.
# History

The Forgers were among the first people to begin experimenting with [[Dulmis' Tears]]. Their founders [[Awakening|Awakened]] through using the tears and we granted ability to Forge artifacts from them. Since then they have expanded their number by [[Awakening]] promising craftsmen and teaching them to Forge artifacts.
# Expansion

## [[Tradorn]]

The Forgers have slowly grown in number while keeping a tight grip on their monopoly within Wagram. With the recent discovery of [[Tradorn]], another[[Bastion Cities|Bastion City]], they have begun making plans to expand their guild outside of [[Wagram]].

The few artifact crafters within [[Tradorn]] are isolated "masters" with no strong organization behind them. The Forgers expect to meet only minor resistance.
## [[Kovati]]

[[Kovati]] is an outpost built by the Forgers as proof of the effectiveness of their [[Shroud Beacon|Shroud Beacons]]. It is the first permanent settlement built outside of a [[Bastion Cities|Bastion City]] to survive more than a few weeks.

Since it's creation less than a year ago the Forgers have been steadily moving their operations into this mountain outpost and out of the control of [[Wagram]]. Already most their Master Forgers live there full time and their Beacon production is now exclusively at the outpost.
# Ranks
## Initiate
%%
### Access
Limited access to common crafting materials and basic texts on magical crafting. Restricted from participating in sensitive guild operations.
### Role
New recruits undergoing probation. They perform menial tasks, learn basic techniques, and demonstrate loyalty to the guild.
### Achievements
Successfully crafting their first enchanted item or proving their dedication through service and skill.
%%
## Apprentice
%%
### Access
Supervised access to mid-tier materials, beginner enchanted tools, and introductory magical formulas. Assigned to a mentor.
### Role
Assists higher-ranking members with projects and continues learning foundational crafting skills. Often tasked with repetitive work that refines basic techniques.
### Achievements
Completing a series of independent crafting tasks or making small improvements to existing designs.
%%
## Crafter
%%
### Access
Standard-quality materials, independent workspace, and access to a shared library of intermediate crafting knowledge.
### Role
Proficient in creating minor magical items and enchantments independently. Contributes to guild operations by producing magical goods or assisting with research.
### Achievements
Demonstrating consistent quality in crafting or collaborating on a significant project for the guild.
%%
## Artisan
%%
### Access
Higher-grade materials, access to specialized crafting equipment, and intermediate-level restricted guild texts.
### Role
Creates advanced magical items and begins experimenting with unique designs. Artisans often act as mentors for Apprentices or oversee small-scale projects.
### Achievements
Designing a successful magical item or process that the guild adopts, or mentoring Apprentices to achieve excellence.
%%
## Adept
%%
### Access
Rare crafting materials, advanced enchanted tools, private workshops, and permission to conduct classified experiments.
### Role
Seasoned crafters with the ability to take on complex and critical projects for the guild. They often manage teams and explore experimental techniques.
### Achievements
Leading a major crafting project or making significant improvements to the guild’s magical crafting methods.
%%
## Innovator
%%
### Access
Premium materials, access to experimental facilities, and clearance for highly classified guild knowledge.
### Role
Pioneers of new techniques and groundbreaking magical designs. Innovators lead research teams and their contributions shape the guild’s future direction.
### Achievements
Developing a revolutionary crafting technique or artifact that solidifies the guild’s monopoly or expands its influence.
%%
## Master Forger
%%
### Access
Unrestricted access to guild knowledge, ancient crafting techniques, and legendary materials.
### Role
Elite crafters responsible for safeguarding and advancing the guild’s most powerful secrets. Masters enforce the guild’s monopoly and maintain its authority.
### Achievements
Achieving unparalleled mastery in magical crafting and playing a pivotal role in suppressing competitors or rivals.
%%
## Grand Forger
%%
### Access
Absolute authority over all guild resources, members, and decisions. Full access to ancient archives and forbidden knowledge.
### Role
The ultimate leader of The Forgers, shaping the guild’s vision and ensuring its monopoly remains unchallenged. The Grand Forger rarely participates directly in crafting but oversees all high-level strategies.
### Achievements
Ascension to the pinnacle of leadership through unmatched contributions, political maneuvering, and an unyielding dedication to the guild's supremacy.
%%
%%
## Whisperwrights
### Access
- **Knowledge:** Restricted access to classified information about the guild’s covert operations, rival factions, and black-market dealings.
- **Resources:** Special funding for covert missions, access to illegal magical materials, and tools tailored for clandestine activities.
- **Contacts:** Networks with external agents, black-market suppliers, and informants.
### Role
Whisperwrights work to eliminate threats to the guild’s monopoly by any means necessary. This includes:

- Spying on rivals or potential competitors.
- Sabotaging unauthorized magical crafting operations.
- Silencing dissidents within or outside the guild.
- Smuggling forbidden materials or items to and from secret locations.
- Bribing or intimidating authorities to protect the guild’s interests.

Their presence within the guild is kept secret from all but the highest-ranking members (Master Forger and above). Those assigned this role must be adept at deception, blending seamlessly into their primary rank while executing their covert duties.

# Creations

While no artifact of power can be called commonplace there are those that fall into general categories that are common amongst artifacts.

These include various weapons that are imbued with power and material that is imbued with extreme durability.

These are things most crafters that have [[Awakening|Awakened]] can achieve early in their development.

The items listed here are specifically advancements in arcane crafting achieved by the Forgers themselves.
## [[Shroud Beacon|Shroud Beacons]]

These arcane devices are able to create permanent pockets of protection from [[The Shroud]] outside of the [[Bastion Cities]]. The secret of their creation is known only to the Forgers.
## [[Piercing Lens]]

Piercing Lens are crafted from the waste material of arcane crafting. While their use is now well known they were first discovered by the Forgers.
