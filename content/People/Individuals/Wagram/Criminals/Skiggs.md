# Summary
The undisputed gang lord of [[The Marsh]]. Almost all of the criminals that live in his district owe some level of loyalty or at least a cut of all profits to him.

A major reason he stays firmly at the top of the pile is his access to [[Dulmis' Tears]] that he gives to certain recruits to bind them both in debt and gratitude to him. This means he has a large number of [[Awakening|Awakened]] working for him.

He himself is known to be [[Awakening|Awakened]] with some abilities related to explosions.
# Connections
### Party
[[Maxir]] was given one of [[Dulmis' Tears]] in exchange for an enormous debt. This led to conflict between Skiggs and the party from the beginning as he sent men to collect his share of their earnings.

The conflict between Skiggs and the party escalated to the point of destroying [[Merrik]]'s home and sending a manhunt for them. While the active hostilities have been settled through 3rd parties that peace is an uneasy one.
### Nobility
Skiggs is actually acting in some way as an agent of [[Gideon Rothvale|Viscount Gideon Rothvale]] who is the noble that is technically responsible for [[The Marsh]] district. He has been supplying Skiggs with [[Dulmis' Tears]].
