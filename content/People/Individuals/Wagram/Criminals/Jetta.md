# Summary
Jetta is a known agent of [[Skiggs]]. She uses her abilities in disguise and mental manipulation for both information gathering and recruitment of new talent for [[Skiggs]].
## Connections
Has encountered the party a couple of times knows all but [[Effi]]'s face.
Seems to have known who [[Merrik]] was before they had a confrontation.
# Powers
## Gilded Mask
The user can subtly shift their appearance and voice to aide in any and all social situations.
%%
- Add +2 to all skill checks that could take into account someone's appearance.
	- Convince, Connect, or even Sneak.
- Ability to change facial features and voice at will
%%
## Silver Tongue
The user can infuse their words with magic. When they speaks with intent, people find it difficult to doubt them or recognize lies, at least in the moment. They can use this to negotiate, trick guards, or diffuse conflicts, but strong-willed individuals may resist, and the effect fades if contradictions become obvious.
%%
- Can be used 3 times per day
- Cause the listener to make a mental saving throw. If they fail they must view the user in whatever way the user wanted as they spoke
	- If the user is pretending to be a commanding officer, or innocent bystander then the victim believes that is what they are.
- If the user takes a hostile action or something completely contradicts the the lie the victim immediately breaks the effect.
%%
## Phantom’s Grace
The user can create illusions of themselves tied to movement. Afterimages of themselves, flickers of motion where their isn’t, or even making an object seem to vanish when they hands it off. This makes them excellent at sleight of hand, escapes, and misdirection.
%%
- Grant 18 AC in combat as they leave behind afterimages and random flickers of motion that make them hard to pin down.
- Once per day they may send out a burst of a dozen images sprinting in all directions in an attempt to escape.
	- Any attack on one of these dozen images requires a roll to determine if it was the real user.
	- 1d12 at the start reducing by one (1d11, 1d10) every time an image is shown to be false by taking damage.
%%
