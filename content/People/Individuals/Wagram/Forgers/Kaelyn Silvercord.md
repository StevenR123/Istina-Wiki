# Summary
Silvercord is an [[Forgers#Adept - Silver|Adept]] of the [[Forgers]] and is based in [[The Shaper’s Haven]], a guildhall near the outer gate in the [[Gate District]].
## Connections
The party has targeted, followed, and secretly read the mind of Silvercord in preparation of a heist/attack on [[The Shaper’s Haven]], but has not yet acted openly.

The party was told by the Forger that caught them spying on [[The Shaper’s Haven]] %%[[Silas]]%% that he would be perfectly happy if they killed Silvercord as long as they left the guildhall alone.
