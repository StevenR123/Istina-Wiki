# Summary
## History

<div hidden>
Nazir is a Dwarven man that was alive before [[Dulmis' Sacrifice]]. He lived in a small nearby settlement to [[Wagram]] that was unaffiliated.

After [[Dulmis' Sacrifice]] he was able to purchase a shop in the lower city as his skills as a blacksmith were needed by the city at large.

He became [[Awakening|Awakened]] only within the last decade and has been honing this new aspect to his craft. He joined the [[Forgers]] in order to receive basic training in Magical Forging, but maintains his individual shop.
</div>
???

## Abilities

One Aspect of Nazir's awakened abilities include the manipulation of Stone. While the other allows him to imbue strength into the items he crafts. He can do so with mundane material at the cost of longevity of the item or can stabilize the imbuement through the use of [[Dulmis' Tears]].
# Notable Encounters
## Robbed
Nazir had his shop broken into by [[Cedric]], [[Effi]], [[Maxir]], and [[Merrik]] after they briefly scouted the location the day before.

He noticed his wards were tripped the day before the robbery and was on alert when it happened. He managed to wound [[Cedric]] before being subdued. He was later forced to unseal his vault at threat of death.

He, along with a guard patrol, were left unconscious and tied up in his shop after the party looted everything easily available in his shop.
